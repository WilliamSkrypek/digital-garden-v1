# Efforts
Below is a list of things I am currently working on or involved in.

2021 - Present  
**Minnesota Youth Symphonies (MYS)**  
A school-year orchestra that meets on Saturday mornings at Macalester College to rehearse professional-level orchestral music for three seasonal concerts. I am currently a member of the Symphony Orchestra, under the direction of Mark Mandarano.

*I also hold the position of Student Representative for the Symphony Orchestra to the MYS student leadership committee*

---
2025 - Present  
**Greater Twin Cities Youth Symphonies (gtcys)**  
Another school-year orchestra that meets weekly on Monday nights at Orchestra Hall, to rehearse symphonies and other orchestral music, for many seasonal and charity concerts. I am currently a member of the Symphony Orchestra, under the direction of Mark Russell Smith.

---
2025, 2026
**Washburn Track & Field**  
Washburn Highschool's track and field team. I run for the Varsity Hurdles team, competing in the 110 meter hurdles and the 300 meter hurdles races.

---
2024 - Present
**Model United Nations**  
A national club, where we explore international diplomacy, and prepare for conferences, in which you represent a country and a policy goal. I participated in the fall conference of 2024 and 2025 in which I represented India and Ethiopia respectively. I also was invited to the spring conference of 2025 as a delegate from Namibia.

**Washburn Site Council**  
The site council at Washburn is an advisory board made up of representatives from all corners of the communities; including teachers, students, parents, and administrators. We cover issues including family outreach, budgetary decisions, student engagement and support, and other needs the community has. We meet monthly on Thursday.
